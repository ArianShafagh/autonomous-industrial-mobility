**Findings you should know about for the thesis:**
- **PPO training bug:** `train_ppo.py:62` uses 4 parallel environments over 6 scenarios, so PPO only trained on 4. It never saw `one_hot_section` or `worn_robot`, but it was evaluated on them.
- **The neural network adds no measurable gain over the rules alone.** On the original 6 scenarios the difference is −0.08 points (95 % CI −1.19 to +1.17). The neuro-symbolic model also does not beat PPO significantly (+0.58, CI includes 0).
- **The safety shield is the strongest result.** The neuro-symbolic model and the rules-only version had 0 safety violations in 240 episodes, including the 6 scenarios the models were never trained on. The network without rules violated safety in 16 of 120 episodes, PPO in 14.
- **One rule gap explains the model's two clear losses** (`worn_robot` and `heavy_load`). No rule stops the robot from waiting away from the charger, and the network keeps picking that. For example, it chose WAIT over DELIVER 14 times with cargo on board.
- **Energy:** the neuro-symbolic model uses more energy than the rule policy in every scenario (+18 % to +252 %). Part of the reason is that the score counts units still in buffers at the end of the shift as zero, so emptying buffers early pays off.
- **The "no AI" fallback rules are a strong baseline** that was never evaluated. They come within 2 % of the neuro-symbolic model in `high_demand`.
- **Smaller bugs:** the `decision_ms` column in `evaluate.py` is always 0, and the WP8 planner benchmark would crash if Nav2 rejected a goal. There are also several outdated docstrings and comments; file 16 lists all of them with line numbers.
